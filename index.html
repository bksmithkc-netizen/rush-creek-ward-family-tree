How to get a GEDCOM file from FamilySearch

You don't need to be a computer expert for this. Just follow each step below, in order. It takes about 15–20 minutes, plus some waiting time while your tree downloads.

  1. Go to ancquest.com and download "Ancestral Quest Basics." It's free, and it works on both Windows and Mac computers.
  2. Open Ancestral Quest. Right on the first screen (or under the "FamilySearch" menu at the top), choose "Import from FamilySearch."
  3. Type in your FamilySearch username and password when it asks. If it offers to back up your tree first, choose Yes.
  4. Choose how many generations back you'd like (8–20 is great). Click Import, then wait — for a large family this can take up to an hour. If it looks frozen or says "FamilySearch is Busy... retrying," that's completely normal. Don't close the program.
  5. When it's done, click "File" at the top, then "Export." Choose GEDCOM as the file type, then save it somewhere easy to find, like your Desktop — name it something like family-tree.ged.
  6. Come back here and upload that file on the "Upload GEDCOM" tab above.

FamilySearch itself confirms it doesn't offer a direct download button for your Family Tree — you always need a helper program like this. Ancestral Quest is one of FamilySearch's official certified partners, so it's a safe, well-tested choice. RootsMagic and Legacy Family Tree are two other well-known certified options if you'd rather try those instead.
